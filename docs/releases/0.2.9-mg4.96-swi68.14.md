# DiPlay-MG4 Plus 0.2.9-mg4.96-swi68.14

Changes since [0.2.9-mg4.96-swi68.11](https://github.com/kennyvv/DiPlay-MG4-Plus/releases/tag/0.2.9-mg4.96-swi68.11).
For SWI68 and SWI165. Installs over swi68.11 (versionCode 137); your settings are kept.

Everything marked **tried in the car** passed a car test on 2026-11-18. The rest is new, off by default, or
only tried in the emulator.

## Highlights

- **Calls sound like the car's own CarPlay.** Calls now play through the car's own phone path, so the head
  unit cancels echo: the other person no longer hears themselves (tried in the car; 13.4 dB less echo than
  before). If that path ever stops taking audio, the call carries on the normal way within half a second
  instead of going silent.
- **The car knows what CarPlay is playing.** Calls hold the car's audio focus for the whole call, the
  iPhone's ringtone plays on the car's ring output at ring volume, Siri is a voice assistant to the car, and
  navigation prompts lower the music, with the volume knob setting prompt volume. All tried in the car.
- **Radio and CarPlay take turns properly.** When the radio or Bluetooth music takes over, the iPhone pauses
  instead of playing into nothing, and pressing play in CarPlay brings the car back to CarPlay. Tried in
  the car.
- **MG4 Launcher support.** DiPlay sends the CarPlay song, cover, play position, phone name and the
  iPhone's battery and charging state to the MG4 Launcher home screen, and takes its play, pause, next and
  previous buttons. Tried in the car.

## Calls (Settings > Sound)

- **Car echo canceller for calls** (on): calls use the car's phone output and phone microphone, as the car's
  own CarPlay does. Tried in the car.
- **Calls get their own audio focus** (on): music stops for the call and comes back after. Tried in the car.
- **Ringtone on the car's ring output** (on): the iPhone's ring plays at ring volume, not with navigation.
  Tried in the car.
- Calls use the plain microphone by default, as the car's own CarPlay does, and the microphone's timing now
  follows the rate the iPhone asks for (from andrew-snape/DiPlay-MG4).
- **Echo suppression on calls** (software, on): still used for calls on the normal path. In the car it did
  not stop the echo on its own; the car's echo canceller above does.
- More call diagnostics in the report: which path a call took, the echo delay, and the hands-free state.

## Siri and navigation (Settings > Sound)

- **Car microphone processing for Siri** (on): Siri records through the car's noise reduction. Tried in the
  car.
- **Siri own audio focus** (on): music drops for Siri and comes back. Tried in the car.
- **Navigation prompts lower music** (on): needs the car's own "lower media for navigation" setting. Tried
  in the car.
- Siri's microphone counts its timing at the rate the iPhone negotiates, which fixed speech with gaps
  (from andrew-snape/DiPlay-MG4).

## CarPlay sound and screen

- **Hand CarPlay audio to the car's sources** (Settings > Sound, on): see Highlights. Tried in the car.
- **Recheck the car's audio source** (Settings > Sound, on): if CarPlay music is silent at connect because
  the car keeps playing another source, DiPlay asks the car again after 2 seconds. Tried in the car.
- **Tell iPhone when CarPlay is hidden (test)** (Settings > Experimental, off): the iPhone stops sending the
  picture while CarPlay is hidden. Worked in the car, but once the picture stayed black for a while on return.

## Dashboard (instrument cluster)

- Map on the dashboard, song on the dashboard, album art and the steering-wheel joystick for CarPlay moved
  from Experimental to Settings > Car. Tried in the car.
- **Pause the dashboard map off its page** (Settings > Car, on): the iPhone stops drawing the dashboard map
  while the cluster shows another page, and draws it again within a second when you go back. Tried in the car.
- The song card shows a progress bar and time ("1:23 / 3:45") and a gentle gradient in the cover's colour.
  Tried in the car.
- **Joystick up and down** and **Joystick left and right** (Normal or Flipped); up and down are flipped by
  default. Tried in the car.
- Turn distances on the cluster follow what CarPlay shows (from andrew-snape/DiPlay-MG4).

## Settings

- New **Developer** page for test tools: Test call echo, Siri prewarm on voice button, Microphone (test),
  Siri microphone as PCM (test), Record microphone (test).
- **Use car's MFi chip (test)** (Experimental, off): authenticates with the car's own Apple chip; the report
  says whether the chip can be used (from andrew-snape/DiPlay-MG4).

## Not yet tried in the car

Siri prewarm, cluster directions on a route, and lane guidance on the dashboard.

## Install

Copy the APK to a USB stick and install it on the head unit as before. If you used a Developer test switch
before, its saved setting is kept; the new defaults apply to settings you never changed.
